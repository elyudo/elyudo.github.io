Lesbianism

Video / Performance

A short film influenced by the new queer media movement in the 90s. In the mockumentary style of Gregg Araki's Totally Fucked Up. Filmed on 2008 Sony Digital Camera.


The piece references underground club culture, ritualised movement, and the aesthetics of collapse and overindulgence.


Presented as a performative study of spectacle, identity and visual intensity.
